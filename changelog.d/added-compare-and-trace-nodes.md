- Added `Compare Images`, which scores how alike two images are and shows where they differ, and `Lines to SVG`,
  which traces the marks in a drawing into an SVG file. The `Compare` widget on `Compare Images` wipes
  between the two images, flips between them, and shows where they differ, with `gain` applied live and
  `preview_size` to raise the detail. The `Trace` widget on `Lines to SVG` shows the traced shapes on your
  image, in the fill colour, and updates when you change the image or a setting.
