- Added `Flatten Paper`, `Lines to Alpha`, `Trim to Content`, `Equalize Contrast` and `Align Images`, which clean up
  and line up photos and scans of drawings. `Lines to Alpha` returns just the alpha channel of the marks, white on a
  mark and black on paper. The `Align` widget on `Align Images` shows the aligned image against the reference: wipe
  between them, flip between them, and see where they differ before and after, with the shift, turn and scale found,
  and `preview_size` to raise the detail.
