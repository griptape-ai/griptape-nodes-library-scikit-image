- Added `Diff Images`, which scores how alike two images are and shows where they differ, and `Lines to SVG`,
  which traces the marks in a drawing into an SVG file. The `Diff` widget on `Diff Images` wipes between
  the two images, flips between them, and shows where they differ, with `gain` applied live and
  `preview_size` to raise the detail. The node saves the view the widget is showing. The `Trace` widget on
  `Lines to SVG` shows the traced shapes on your image, in the fill colour, and updates when you change the
  image or a setting.
